Emergency Evacuation
Know your nearest emergency exits
📋 Evacuation Procedures

1. Stay Calm: Follow instructions from faculty or emergency personnel.

2. Use Nearest Exit: Proceed to the closest safe exit. Do not use elevators.

3. Assembly Point: Gather at the open field near Main Gate.

4. Account for Everyone: Faculty will conduct headcount.

5. Wait for Instructions: Do not re-enter until authorized.

📞 Emergency Contacts

Campus Security: (043) 756-5555 local 101

Medical Clinic: (043) 756-5555 local 201

Lipa City Emergency: 911

Fire Department: (043) 756-1234
