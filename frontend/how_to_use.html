Getting Started in 5 Easy Steps

1

Access SPARTA

Tap "Talk to SPARTA" from the main menu. SPARTA is available 24/7 on any device — phone, tablet, or computer.

Tip: Bookmark the page for quick access anytime!
2

Type Your Question

Just type in plain language — no special commands needed. SPARTA understands casual conversation.

Example: "Where is Room 301?" or "What are the admission requirements for Engineering?"
3

Get Instant Answers

SPARTA responds right away with accurate, helpful information. Ask follow-up questions anytime.

Follow-up: "Can you tell me more?" or "How do I get there?"
4

Explore the 3D Map

Tap "Navigate the Campus" to explore the interactive 3D campus map — find buildings, rooms, and plan your route.

Try: Searching for "Library" or "College of Engineering" to see directions.
5

Use Voice Input

Tap the 🎤 microphone button and speak your question naturally. SPARTA supports both English and Tagalog.

Say: "Saan ang library?" or "Who is the dean of COE?"

Pro Tips

🎯 Be Specific

The more specific your question, the better the answer. Try "Where is the COE Dean's office?" instead of just "dean".

🌐 Switch Languages

Tap the EN|TL toggle in the chatbot header to switch between English and Tagalog in one tap.

⚡ Quick Questions

Swipe the chip row below the chat to see popular quick-questions. Tap any chip to send instantly.

🔔 Announcements

Tap the bell icon on the main menu to see the latest campus announcements and events.
