💬 Talking to SPARTA

SPARTA's chatbot answers questions about admissions, offices, faculty, events, and more — in plain English or Tagalog, 24/7.

loginGetting to the Chatbot

1

Open the Main Menu

Launch SPARTA in your browser. You'll land on the main menu with quick-access tiles.

2

Tap "Talk to SPARTA"

This opens the chat window — no login or account needed. Works on phone, tablet, or desktop.

💡 Bookmark the page for one-tap access anytime

chat_bubbleAsking a Question

keyboard

Type Naturally

No commands or keywords required — just ask like you would ask a person.

mic

Use Voice Input

Tap the mic icon and speak your question. SPARTA transcribes and answers automatically.

bolt

Try Quick Questions

Tap a suggestion chip under the chat box (e.g. "Who is the dean?") to send it instantly.

Where is the speech lab?
The Speech Lab is on the 2nd floor of the CTE Building, near the Faculty Room. Want directions on the map?
Saan ang library?
Ang Library ay matatagpuan sa Main Building, Ground Floor, tabi ng Registrar's Office.

translateVoice & Language Controls

mic

Microphone Button

Tap the 🎤 icon in the input bar, allow microphone access when prompted, then speak clearly. Recognition matches your current language setting.

EN｜TL

Language Toggle

Tap the EN | TL pill in the header to switch between English and Tagalog with one tap — this affects both chat replies and voice recognition/speech.

volume_up

Text-to-Speech

SPARTA can read its answers aloud. A "Speaking…" indicator appears while it talks — tap the ✕ on that indicator to stop at any time.

campaignStaying Updated with Announcements

SPARTA doesn't rely on asking the chatbot for news — announcements are pushed to you automatically through the pop-up announcement system, available on every page.

notifications_active

Automatic Pop-up

New announcements appear in a card as soon as you open SPARTA — no need to ask.

notifications

A floating bell button stays on screen. Tap it anytime to reopen announcements you've dismissed.

swipe

tips_and_updates

🗺️ Using the Campus Navigator

An interactive 3D map of BSU Lipa — search any room or building, get walking directions, and find emergency exits.

loginGetting to the Navigator

1

From the Main Menu

Tap "Navigate the Campus" on the main menu — or tap the map icon inside the chatbot header.

2

Let the Map Load

The 3D campus model loads automatically. You'll see a loading indicator briefly while it initializes.

searchSearching for a Location

search

Type in the Search Bar

Use the search box at the top of the sidebar (desktop) or the floating search bar on the map (mobile). Start typing a room or building name — results appear live as you type.

layers

Filter by Floor

Use the floor pills (e.g. "All Floors", "Floor 1", "Floor 2") to narrow results to a specific level of a building.

place

Select a Result

Tap a location from the results list. The map flies to that spot and opens an info card with details.

directionsGetting Directions

1

Open a Location's Info Card

Search for or tap your destination on the map to open its info panel.

2

Tap "Get Directions"

SPARTA draws an animated golden path from the Main Entrance Gate to your destination.

3

Check the Route Info Card

See estimated distance and walking time at a glance before you head out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use SPARTA? +
No. Both the chatbot and the campus navigator are open to anyone — no sign-up or login required.
Does voice input work in Tagalog? +
Yes. Switch the language toggle to TL before tapping the microphone, and SPARTA will listen and reply in Tagalog.
What if my browser doesn't support voice input? +
You can always type your question instead — every chatbot feature is fully usable with text alone.
Can I use the Navigator to find the nearest exit during an emergency? +
Yes, the Evacuation tab shows nearby emergency exits and routes. Treat it as a helpful reference — always prioritize posted signage and staff instructions in a real emergency.
Does SPARTA work on mobile? +
Yes, both the chatbot and the 3D navigator are fully responsive, with touch-friendly controls like pinch-to-zoom and a bottom navigation bar on the map.
