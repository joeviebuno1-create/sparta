What is SPARTA?

SPARTA is an intelligent campus companion portal for Batangas State University — The National Engineering University, Lipa Campus. It helps students and visitors navigate the campus with a 3D interactive map, get instant answers through a RAG-powered chatbot, and stay informed with real-time announcements.
