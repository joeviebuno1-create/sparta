Intent Health Score
Identifies undertrained intents and dead configurations before they hurt student experience.
What each status means
check_circle Healthy
Confidence ≥80% and fallback rate <15%. The intent is well-trained and reliably understood by the chatbot.
warning Needs Work
Confidence 60–79% or fallback rate 15–40%. The intent is partially trained — add more keyword examples to improve accuracy.
error High Fallback
Fallback rate >40%. The chatbot frequently cannot match this intent — responses are being missed or defaulted. Needs immediate retraining.
block Dead Config
Zero or near-zero triggers. The intent exists but is never matched. Either it is misconfigured, no users ask about this topic, or its keywords conflict with another intent.
Avg Conf. — average match confidence (0–100%). Higher is better.   Fallback % — percentage of triggers where the chatbot gave a generic fallback response instead of the specific intent response. Lower is better.
